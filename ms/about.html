About Us

We are a specialized engineering team dedicated to protecting your digital privacy as simply and effectively as possible. We created Temp Mail to resolve an everyday challenge faced by virtually every web user: the requirement to supply an email address when accessing unverified services, without exposing your permanent personal inbox to relentless spam, intrusive tracking, or unexpected data breaches.

What Sets Us Apart?

  • Instant disposable email generation with zero registration and zero personal credentials needed.
  • Real-time message reception and automatic OTP code detection with 1-click copying.
  • Complete automated purge cycle: inboxes and messages are permanently erased after 20 minutes.
  • Hardened encryption architecture that strictly isolates every generated mailbox.

Our Commitment

We never sell your data, never display tracking advertisements, and never demand identity verification. Our singular goal is providing you with a clean, fast, and dependable tool that safeguards your personal email address.
