Frequently Asked Questions

Is this temporary email service completely free?

Yes, 100% free with no hidden fees, no subscriptions, no ads, and absolutely zero personal registration required.

How long does each temporary inbox remain active?

Each session lasts for 20 minutes by default. You can extend it by 10 minutes at any time, or click "Burn Mailbox" to destroy all records instantly.

Can I receive verification codes (OTP) and activation links?

Yes, our server-side engine parses MIME emails to extract OTP numeric/alphanumeric codes and direct activation links with one-click copy buttons.

How do you guarantee user isolation and privacy?

We employ deterministic O(1) hashing that routes each email to an isolated Cloudflare Durable Object protected by an individual secret bearer token. No third-party email providers or analytics are used.
