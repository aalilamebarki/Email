Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: September 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We require no personal information to access or use the service. Temporary email addresses are generated entirely at random and are never linked to your legal name, real email address, IP identity, or personal identifiers.

2. Received Messages & Automated Purging

Incoming emails delivered to your temporary address are retained in ephemeral memory for a maximum duration of 20 minutes, after which they are automatically, irrevocably destroyed. We do not maintain backups or archives of expired mailboxes.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We strictly use browser local storage (localStorage) exclusively for functional preferences: preserving your selected visual theme (dark/light) and preferred language. We do not deploy behavioral tracking cookies, pixels, or third-party ad beacons.

4. Zero Third-Party Data Sharing & Private Infrastructure

We never monetize, sell, license, or exchange user session data with third parties or data brokers. No third-party email services of any kind are used; all messages are processed and stored exclusively within our private Cloudflare infrastructure.

5. Terms of Service & Disclaimer

Because temporary email addresses are disposable by design, they must not be used for illicit purposes, phishing, automated abuse, or critical lifelong accounts (e.g., banking or primary authentication). Users bear sole responsibility for utilizing the tool within its intended disposable scope.
