Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, clear answers on how temporary disposable email works, security standards, and privacy protection.

Is temporary email safe to use? ▼

Yes, for temporary and non-sensitive tasks such as website signups, trials, or receiving verification codes. We do not recommend using it for long-term critical accounts or confidential personal data.

How long are emails stored before deletion? ▼

Messages and active addresses are automatically deleted after 20 minutes and cannot be recovered afterward, ensuring total privacy.

Can I use it to receive OTP verification codes? ▼

Yes, this is one of the most common use cases. Our engine automatically detects OTP codes and highlights them with an instant one-click copy button.

Does it require registration or personal details? ▼

Not at all. Your disposable address is generated immediately when opening the page without any sign-up or personal information required.

Can I change my temporary email address? ▼

Yes, you can generate a new random address at any time with a single click using the "Change Address" button.
