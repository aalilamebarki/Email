What is Temporary Email?

Temporary email (also known as disposable email, temp mail, or burner email) is a self-destructing email address generated in seconds and automatically deleted after a short period. It gives you an anonymous inbox to receive messages without ever revealing your personal or corporate email.

  3. 3. Receive Emails Instantly:

    Incoming messages arrive in your live inbox via WebSockets without requiring manual refreshes.

Is Temporary Email Secure?

Yes, as long as it is used for its intended purpose: short-term registrations and non-sensitive communications. We do not recommend using disposable emails for primary banking, government services, or long-term critical accounts because expired addresses cannot be recovered.
